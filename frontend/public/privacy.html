SAGE privacy

SAGE (Student Advisor for Guided Enrolment) is a final-year engineering project at the University of Cape Town. It helps UCT students plan their electives. This page says what it keeps about you.

What SAGE stores

  • Your UCT email address, which is your SAGE account and contains your student number, and the identifiers Microsoft gives that account. Supabase, SAGE's sign-in service, keeps these. SAGE needs your email to know who you are, and to check that a transcript you upload is yours.
  • The course history you confirm from your transcript.
  • Your plans, and your answers to the planning questionnaire.
  • The electives you heart.
  • Your questions to the advisor, and its replies.

Supabase keeps this in the European Union. SAGE itself runs on Microsoft Azure in South Africa.

What SAGE's own records never hold

  • Your name. When you sign in, your browser asks Microsoft for it and keeps it on that device only, until you sign out.
  • Your student number, apart from inside your email address above. SAGE works it out from your email whenever it needs it.
  • Your transcript. SAGE reads it in memory to fill in your course history, then discards it.

Withdrawing your consent

The first time you signed in, Microsoft asked you to let SAGE sign you in and read your profile. To withdraw that, open My Apps, hover over SAGE, select the ellipsis (…), choose Manage your application, then Revoke Permissions. If you sign in to SAGE again later, Microsoft asks for your consent again.

Withdrawing consent doesn't delete what SAGE stores. To have that deleted, see below.

Deleting your data

To have your SAGE account and everything stored with it deleted, email the responsible person from your UCT address. Deleting your account removes all your SAGE data with it.
